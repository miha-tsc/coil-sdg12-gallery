UN Sustainable Development Goal 12

Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns

SDG 12 calls for “doing more and better with less”, decoupling economic growth from environmental degradation, improving quality of life, and reducing the ecological footprint of our goods, food, energy and waste — now and for future generations.

The 12.x Targets

Goal 12 is made up of science-backed targets covering waste, pollution, natural-resource management, corporate reporting and sustainable public procurement.

12.1

10-Year Framework

Implement the 10-Year Framework of Programmes on Sustainable Consumption and Production Patterns, all countries taking action.

12.2

Natural Resources

Achieve the sustainable management and efficient use of natural resources by 2030.

12.3

Food Waste

Halve per-capita global food waste at retail and consumer level, and reduce food losses along production and supply chains.

12.4

Chemicals & Waste

Achieve environmentally sound management of chemicals and all wastes throughout their life cycle, reducing releases to air, water and soil.

12.5

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle

Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse.

12.6

Corporate Reporting

Encourage companies — especially large and transnational ones — to adopt sustainable practices and integrate sustainability into their reports.

12.7

Public Procurement

Promote sustainable public procurement practices following policies and priorities.

12.8

Education for All

Ensure people everywhere have the information and awareness for sustainable development and lifestyles in harmony with nature.

12.a

Support Developing Countries

Strengthen scientific and technological capacity for developing countries to move toward more sustainable consumption and production.

12.b

Sustainable Tourism

Develop and implement tools to monitor sustainable tourism that creates jobs and promotes local culture and products.

12.c

Fossil-Fuel Subsidies

Rationalize inefficient fossil-fuel subsidies that encourage wasteful consumption by removing market distortions.

Take Action

Small everyday choices scale into systemic change. Start with these:

  • 🛒 Buy only what you need — plan meals and shopping lists.
  • ♻️ Follow the hierarchy: Refuse → Reduce → Reuse → Recycle → Rot.
  • 🍽️ Compost food scraps and store leftovers wisely.
  • 👕 Choose quality over quantity; repair, swap and buy second-hand.
  • ⚡ Switch to efficient appliances and renewable energy providers.
  • 💧 Save water — shorter showers, full loads, fix leaks.
  • 🏷️ Support brands with transparent, sustainable supply chains.
  • 📣 Speak up — vote, advocate and share what you learn.
